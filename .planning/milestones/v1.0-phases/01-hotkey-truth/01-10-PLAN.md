<objective>
Close the phase in the ledger, honestly — including the one requirement whose premise dissolved.

Purpose: HOTKEY-10 asks to *"re-key the envelope's `libkeybinder` hardness re-measure trigger to a
version the tree can actually observe changing, since `hotkey_manager_linux` is transitive and only
the facade is pinned."* **That trigger no longer has a subject.** Its clause
(`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:584`) says "Re-measure when the plugin version moves", about
`hotkey_manager_linux 0.2.0` — a package `pubspec.lock` records as `dependency: transitive`, reached
through the facade's own `^0.2.0` constraint. Plan 01-02 deleted the facade, which takes all four
packages out of the lock. And the claim the trigger guarded is retired too: the hardness clause exists
because a library the app cannot supply is resolved before `main()`, and under the chosen route
`libkeybinder-3.0-0` leaves the runtime-dependency list outright while its replacement `libX11.so.6`
is already an unconditional `DT_NEEDED` of `libgdk-3.so.0` on every host.

So the requirement is closed **with that finding**, not by inventing a re-keying that has nothing to
point at. That is a real deliverable — a recorded dissolution plus the envelope amendment it implies —
and it is the honest one.

The rest of this plan is the phase's closure record: nine entries closed with file:line evidence, and
four new entries filed for work this phase deliberately did not do.

Output: an append-only ledger that tells the truth about what this phase closed, what it superseded,
and what it handed on.
