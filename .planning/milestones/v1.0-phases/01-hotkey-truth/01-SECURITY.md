## How this file was produced — read before trusting a `closed`

Every one of the 82 threats below is recorded `closed`, and **63 of them are closed on
the authoring plan's own claim that a control exists, not on an audit that went and looked.**
That is a deliberate, human-made decision taken on 2026-09-11 at the `/gsd-secure-phase 01`
Step 4 gate: the alternative offered was to spawn `gsd-security-auditor` in verify-mitigations
mode against the implementation, and it was declined in favour of accepting the register as
written.

The distinction matters on this phase specifically, and the reason is on the record. DW-123
exists because DW-9, DW-26 and DW-44 were all closed citing a premise — "this container has no
compositor, no xdg-desktop-portal, no session bus and no reachable X display" — that later turned
out to be false, and that false closure is what cost the phase gap G-01-13. A plan asserting its
own mitigation is the same shape of evidence. So:

- `closed (accepted)` means a human accepted the risk with the rationale recorded here.
- `closed (plan-claimed)` means the plan states a control and nobody verified it independently.
  It is **not** the same as a verified mitigation, and it should not be read as one.

To convert the plan-claimed rows into verified ones, re-run `/gsd-secure-phase 01` and choose
"Verify all open threats". Nothing in this file has to be edited first — the auditor re-derives
status from the implementation.
