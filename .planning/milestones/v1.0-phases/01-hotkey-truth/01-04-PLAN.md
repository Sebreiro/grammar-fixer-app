<objective>
Split `HotkeyUnavailable`'s three distinct meanings so a consumer can tell them apart without parsing
a message string, and render them as the three distinguishable messages D-06 asks for.

Purpose: one variant carrying one free-text `message` now means three different things — *no backend
at all* (a wlroots compositor with no GlobalShortcuts portal), *a working backend refused this one
key*, and *a live binding was revoked by the desktop*. A consumer cannot tell "never retry" from
"retry with a different key" from "you just lost the one you had", and the only carrier is prose.
That is FLAT-04, filed by the adversarial lens and by the architecture Reviewer Gate across two
passes.

This edits AD-9's verbatim-frozen field list at spine lines 274–278, which is a **one-way door** and
is gated at the head of this plan.

Output: a defaultless `cause` on `HotkeyUnavailable`, every construction site naming one, the portal
refusal type carrying one out of the four-deep handshake, and a settings screen that switches on the
cause instead of the sentence.
</objective>
