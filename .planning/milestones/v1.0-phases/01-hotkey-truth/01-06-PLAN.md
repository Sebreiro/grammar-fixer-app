<objective>
Make the settings screen show **what is in effect** rather than what was asked for, on both display
servers, and give it a way to read that synchronously when it mounts.

Purpose: two defects with one shape. `GlobalHotkey.bindingChanges` is a broadcast stream with no
replay and the port offers no synchronous accessor, so a compositor rebind that arrives while the
settings screen is closed is dropped and AD-10 then renders the stale startup `bind()` result — the
ordinary tray-daemon case, since the screen is almost never mounted (FLAT-02). And on Wayland,
`effective` is `null` at all three construction sites (`:342`, `:874`, `:971`), so AD-10's *display*
rule is honoured by a sentence — *"This backend cannot report the combination in effect"* — rather
than by a read-back (DW-66).

**The read-back is real; the parse is not.** No field of any GlobalShortcuts reply carries a
machine-readable combination: `description` and `trigger_description` are the only shortcut properties
in the `BindShortcuts` Response, the `ListShortcuts` reply and the `ShortcutsChanged` signal. So
HOTKEY-03's amendment (D-04, D-05) is a faithful reading of the protocol rather than a concession —
the app reads the portal's report instead of echoing its own request, and displays that report
verbatim. DW-66's own `decision:` line (2026-08-14, ledger 1136) already ratified exactly this:
*"Render the localized description — the portal's trigger_description is displayed on the settings
screen, clearly labelled as the desktop's own wording rather than this app's. Parsing the phrase back
was rejected as silently wrong on a translated string."*

Output: the port member ratified in plan 01-01, both adapters answering it, the portal's own text
carried out of the adapter instead of only logged, and a settings screen with no regime label.
</objective>
