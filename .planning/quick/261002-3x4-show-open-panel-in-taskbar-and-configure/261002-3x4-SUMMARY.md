# Open panel in taskbar and configurable native Close

The warm panel is eligible for the desktop taskbar/dock when visible. Startup remains hidden. Settings offers When closing the window: Close to tray (default) or Quit app. The preference writes through to config.json, survives restarts, and follows external config edits. Existing config files adopt the tray default.

Native Close reports intent through the visibility port. PanelCloseController applies the committed preference and emits one quit request when needed. Main joins window and tray quit into the same ordered shutdown, with pending history writes drained before exit. Hotkey and focus-loss dismissals continue to hide independently of the close preference.
